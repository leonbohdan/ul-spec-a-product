Hey team,

I've been thinking... we're getting feedback all over the place – emails, Twitter DMs, that chat widget on the site, sometimes even LinkedIn messages. It's kind of a mess and I'm not sure we're capturing everything or responding to the right stuff.

What if we had like... a place where customers could just post their ideas? I'm thinking something public maybe? So other customers can see what people are asking for? Not sure if they should be able to comment or just vote on things they want too.

Also I think it would be good if we could keep track of what we're actually working on vs what we're just thinking about. Like some kind of status thing? But I don't want customers to have to create accounts or anything – that seems like too much friction.

Oh and obviously we'd need to see who submitted what, but maybe not publicly? For follow-up emails and stuff.

Anyway, just an idea. Let me know what you think or if this even makes sense.
